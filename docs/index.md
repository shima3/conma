# ConMa

ConMa is an intermediate language that provides higher-order functions and continuations for functional programming.

## Files that moved

Files and directories have been moved and renamed. A text that describes the present (a README, the specification, the design, the comments of the code) is changed to the new place. A text that records its own time (the reviews, the plans, the steps of the roadmap) keeps the old path and adds a note after it: "(moved to `NEW` on 2026-11-15)", or "(moved on DATE; now `NEW`)" for a file that moved more than once, and "(removed on DATE)" for one that was removed, with where its content went if it went somewhere; in Japanese, 「（2026-11-15 に NEW へ移動）」, 「（DATE に移動。今は NEW）」 and 「（DATE に削除）」. The date is that of the move. Line numbers in such a text are those of its time, and are not changed. The records of docs/TESTS-ja.md name the commit, and get no notes.
